# Contributing to ARIADNE

ARIADNE welcomes evidence, questions, corrections, proposals, and results that help develop the project. Do not share confidential, personal, restricted, or embargoed information.

## Ways to contribute

- Use the [ledger contribution form](../../issues/new?template=ledger-contribution.yml) to propose a project record.
- Use the [general issue form](../../issues/new?template=general.yml) for questions, typos, broken links, or unclear wording.
- Use a pull request for substantial document changes.
- Core editors may make focused edits directly when appropriate.

Keep each contribution clear, focused, and supported by evidence where possible.

## Provenance ledger

The ledger distinguishes:

- **Observed:** supported by available evidence;
- **User-reported:** reported by a participant;
- **Proposal:** suggested but not agreed;
- **Decision:** confirmed by the relevant authority;
- **Result:** completed work with supporting evidence;
- **Correction:** a change to an earlier entry.

```text
### YYYY-MM-DD — Short title

**Type:** Observed | User-reported | Proposal | Decision | Result | Correction
**Claim:** One specific statement.
**Evidence:** A link, repository path, meeting record, or "pending".
**Recorded by:** Name or role.
**Supersedes:** Earlier entry, or "none".
```

A ledger entry records the project's development; it does not turn a proposal into an approved decision. Correct or replace earlier claims through a new entry rather than deleting them.

## Review

Contributions are reviewed for clarity, relevance, evidence, confidentiality, and appropriate decision authority. Accepted changes should be linked to the resulting ledger entry or commit.
